Processing and conversion
-------------------------

round(clip(x, -1, 1) * 32767). No volume normalization, silence trimming,
or noise removal was applied. The source WAV is 24 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM,
1.36 seconds long:
SHA-256: a98dae251f4153d028338319ef5217d2215d7a0db01e09682108c820d3a8999b

The full utterance was resampled to 16 kHz mono signed 16-bit little-endian
(s16le) raw PCM using FFmpeg 8.0.1-3ubuntu2, libswresample 6.1.100:

ffmpeg -y -hide_banner -loglevel error -i warmup-good-morning.wav \
  -map_metadata -1 -ac 1 -ar 16000 -c:a pcm_s16le \
  -fflags +bitexact -flags:a +bitexact -f s16le \
  warmup_speech_16khz_mono_s16le.pcm
