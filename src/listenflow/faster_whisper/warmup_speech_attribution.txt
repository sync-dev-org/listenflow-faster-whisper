Modifications
-------------

The repository fixture-generation script converted the librosa OGG source
with ffmpeg to a 16 kHz, mono, signed 16-bit little-endian (s16le) PCM WAVE.
The packaged `warmup_speech_16khz_mono_s16le.pcm` is an exact 1.000-second
crop of that conversion, starting at offset 0.250 seconds (sample 4,000) and
ending immediately before offset 1.250 seconds (16,000 samples, 32,000
bytes). The samples were not resampled or otherwise altered after the crop.
